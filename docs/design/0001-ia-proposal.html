Fuel — where each thing belongs

You were right: everything landed on Today. This is the audit, what the leading apps do instead, and exactly what I propose moving — drawn, so we can argue about it before any code changes.

1. The problem, measured

Today can render 9 competing blocks on a logged day. Measured on a real account at 390×890, a single ordinary day was already 1,065 px of content — and that was with only one of the conditional cards showing.

MyFitnessPal did this in April 2026: replaced its Diary tab with a card-based Today tab. Version rating fell 3.24 → 1.54 stars. The top complaint was not that the cards were ugly — it was “the food diary is now buried.”

3. Trends + Report become one Progress tab

Every competitor has exactly one “how am I doing over time” destination — Progress, Discover, Insights, Analysis. Fuel has two, and neither is complete alone: Trends holds the charts, Report holds the verdict and the target proposal. Merging them frees a tab slot.

5. The rules this sets

  • Today holds at most 5 blocks. A new feature doesn’t get added to Today — it displaces something, or it lives elsewhere.
  • At most one moment at a time. Never two banners stacked.
  • Two disclosure levels, never three (Nielsen Norman: past two levels “users often get lost”).
  • Today’s meals never moves further away. That’s the exact mistake that cost MyFitnessPal 1.7 stars.

6. Two things I want your call on

Water on Today, or into the detail sheet? It’s a daily action, not a statistic, so I lean to keeping it — but it’s the weakest of the five blocks.

Streak as a header chip, or off Today entirely? The evidence mildly favours removing it. The chip is my compromise: present, not competing.
